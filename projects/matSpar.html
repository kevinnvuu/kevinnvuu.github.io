MatSpar

A smart app for meal planning, budgeting, and reducing food waste.

View Full Figma Prototype

Role

Project leader and UX/UI Designer

Tools

Figma, Miro, Illustrator

Overview

MatSpar is a mobile app designed to help students and families manage meal planning, reduce food waste, and stay within their grocery budget. The app combines smart meal suggestions, automatic shopping lists, and budget tracking in one simple and user-friendly solution. This project was developed as part of an 8-week group project, where I focused on information architecture, wireframes, and interactive prototyping in Figma.

The Problem

Many students and families struggle to keep track of groceries, plan meals efficiently, and avoid unnecessary food waste. Existing solutions often focus only on recipes or shopping lists, making meal planning feel fragmented and time-consuming. The goal of MatSpar was to create a more seamless experience that helps users save money and simplify everyday food planning.

Process

The project followed a Lean UX and design thinking approach, combining user research, ideation, prototyping, and usability testing. We conducted semi-structured interviews to better understand users’ habits and frustrations around meal planning and grocery shopping.

Based on our findings, we developed low-fidelity wireframes and user flows in Figma to explore navigation and core features early in the process. After several iterations, we created an interactive prototype focused on simplicity, clear information hierarchy, and intuitive navigation.

We also conducted usability testing with five participants to evaluate the user experience and identify improvement areas. Insights from testing helped refine the shopping list flow, recipe customization, and the visibility of budget-saving features.

Result

The final solution combined meal planning, shopping lists, and budget management into one cohesive experience. User testing showed that participants found the app intuitive and especially appreciated the weekly meal overview and automatic shopping list features.

Through this project, I gained valuable experience in user-centered design, prototyping, usability testing, and iterative design processes. It also strengthened my ability to design clear and practical digital solutions based on real user needs.
